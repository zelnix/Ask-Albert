# Ask Albert product boundary

Ask Albert is permanently **simulation-only**. Paper Autopilot makes automatic simulated trades in the app's paper ledger, never real orders. Public CoinGecko, Kraken and Coinbase connections are market-data feeds only. Exchange-account connections, live spot trading, exchange order limits and exchange certification are **not on the roadmap**.

The frozen top-50 candidates plus the original assets are checked by canonical asset identity and available data. A supported strategy can Start on WAIT. New simulated buys need a current usable mark, the indicators that its authoritative strategy actually requires, and existing mandate/risk checks. Missing data never becomes a fabricated price, candle or fill; valid existing exits still need a real usable price. Existing allocations and ledger history are immutable.
